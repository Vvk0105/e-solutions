DOOR CLOSER TYPES

With Hold Open

A door closer with hold-open is a mechanical device that automatically closes a door but also has a mechanism to keep it temporarily open at a specific angle until manually released or pulled shut. Ideal for high-traffic areas requiring temporary open positions.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Without Hold Open

A door closer without hold-open is a mechanical device that automatically closes a door after it's opened, consistently bringing it back to the closed position without any mechanism to keep it ajar. Provides reliable, consistent closing action.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish
