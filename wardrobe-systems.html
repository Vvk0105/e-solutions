SLIDING WARDROBE SYSTEMS

Sliding/swing wardrobe

Aluminium sliding wardrobes feature doors that glide horizontally on tracks, saving space and offering a sleek, modern look. Aluminium swing wardrobes have hinged doors that open outwards, providing full access to the wardrobe's interior and a more traditional aesthetic. Both offer durability and a contemporary feel with their aluminium construction.

Cupboard

An aluminium cupboard is a durable and lightweight storage unit constructed with aluminium, offering a modern, sleek aesthetic and resistance to rust and pests.
