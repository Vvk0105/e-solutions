PEEPHOLE OPTIONS

Standard Peephole

A peephole is a small lens-fitted opening in a door, allowing a person inside to view who is outside without opening the door. Our standard models provide clear viewing with privacy protection.

Wide-Angle Peephole

Enhanced wide-angle peepholes provide a broader field of view, allowing you to see more of the area outside your door. Ideal for security-conscious installations where maximum visibility is desired.

Digital Peephole

Modern digital peepholes combine traditional functionality with electronic features like video recording and night vision. Some models connect to smartphones for remote viewing capabilities.
