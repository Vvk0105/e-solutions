Elevator control and DND system

A system that manages the movement and operation of elevators, directing them efficiently to respond to calls and transport passengers, allows guests to signal their privacy preference (e.g., Do Not Disturb or Make Up Room), typically through an in-room control panel that illuminates an indicator outside the door.

Biometric digital door lock

A biometric digital door lock is an electronic lock that grants access using unique biological characteristics (like fingerprints or facial recognition), offering keyless entry and enhanced security.
