High-security mechanical and electronic solutions

LOCKS

Mortise lock

A mortise lock is a highly secure lock that is installed by cutting a pocket (mortise) into the edge of a door, with the lock body entirely concealed within, offering a sleek appearance and robust protection.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Dead lock

A deadbolt (often just called a deadlock) is a type of door lock that operates independently of the handle mechanism, using a solid, rectangular bolt that extends deep into the door frame for enhanced security, only operable with a key or thumb-turn.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Hook lock

A hook lock is a type of security lock featuring a curved, hook-shaped bolt that engages with a strike plate, commonly used for sliding doors or gates to prevent them from being pried open.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Cylinder

Component that houses the tumblers and keyway, which the correct key rotates to activate the locking mechanism.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Tower bolt

A tower bolt is a simple, manually operated sliding bolt typically used for securing doors, gates, or windows from one side, offering basic but effective latching.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Concealed flush bolt

A concealed flush bolt is a door bolt that is hidden from view when installed, typically within the edge of an inactive door leaf in a double-door setup, providing discreet and secure locking.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish
