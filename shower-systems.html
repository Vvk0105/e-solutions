Sliding Shower System

A glass sliding shower system features frameless or framed glass panels that glide horizontally on a track to create a shower enclosure, ideal for saving space and providing a sleek, modern bathroom aesthetic.

  • Brushed Stainless Steel
  • (Custom finishes available)

Glass Options:

  • Tempered Clear
  • Frosted

  • Laminated
