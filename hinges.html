HINGE SYSTEMS

Premium door hardware engineered for durability and smooth operation

HINGES

4 ball bearing

4 ball bearing hinges are door hinges that incorporate four sets of ball bearings between their knuckles, significantly reducing friction and ensuring smoother, quieter, and more durable operation, especially for heavy or frequently used doors.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

2 ball bearing

2 ball bearing hinges are door hinges that feature two sets of ball bearings within their knuckles, designed to reduce friction and provide smoother operation than standard hinges, suitable for moderately used doors.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Butterfly hinge

Butterfly hinges are small, decorative hinges with leaves resembling a butterfly's wings, often used for lightweight applications like cabinets, boxes, or small gates, combining functionality with a distinct aesthetic.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish

Soft closing hinge

Soft-closing hinges are door hinges equipped with an integrated dampening mechanism that slows the closing action, preventing doors (especially cabinet doors) from slamming shut and ensuring a gentle, quiet close.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish
