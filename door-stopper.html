DOOR STOPPER SYSTEMS

Protective solutions to prevent door damage and maintain wall integrity

DOOR STOPPER TYPES

Floor Mounted

Floor mounted door stoppers are installed directly into the floor surface, providing a sturdy and permanent solution to prevent doors from swinging too far. Available in various finishes to match your interior design.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Stainless Steel Finish

Wall Mounted

Wall mounted door stoppers attach to the baseboard or wall, protecting both the door and wall from damage. These discreet solutions come in various styles and finishes to blend seamlessly with your decor.

Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Satin Stainless Steel Finish
