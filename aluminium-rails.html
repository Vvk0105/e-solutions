Lightweight yet durable railing solutions with premium finishes for coastal and high-rise applications.

ALUMINIUM RAILS

Continuous railing system

A continuous aluminium railing system is a seamless, uninterrupted railing made of aluminium, designed to provide continuous support and a sleek, modern aesthetic along stairs, balconies, or walkways, with minimal visible joints.

Frame Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Aluminium Mill Finish
  • Silver Finish
  • (Custom finishes available)

Glass Options:

  • Single Glazed (Toughened /Tempered)
  • Laminated
  • Frosted
  • Performance
  • Curved Glass

Bracket railing system

An aluminium bracket railing system is a railing assembly where aluminium support or glass panels are secured using visible or concealed aluminium brackets, offering a robust and often modular design with a modern look.

Frame Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Aluminium Mill Finish
  • Silver Finish
  • (Custom finishes available)

Glass Options:

  • Single Glazed (Toughened /Tempered)
  • Laminated
  • Frosted
  • Performance
  • Curved Glass

Concealed railing system

A concealed aluminium railing system is a modern railing where the aluminium posts or fixings are hidden within the structure or fascia, creating a sleek, minimalist appearance with the glass or top rail seemingly floating without visible supports.

Frame Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Aluminium Mill Finish
  • Silver Finish
  • (Custom finishes available)

Glass Options:

  • Single Glazed (Toughened /Tempered)
  • Laminated
  • Frosted
  • Performance
  • Curved Glass

Fascia mount railing system

A fascia mount aluminium railing system is a railing where the aluminium posts or supports are mounted directly to the side of a deck, balcony, or slab (the fascia), rather than on top, maximizing usable floor space and offering a clean, contemporary look.

Frame Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Aluminium Mill Finish
  • Silver Finish
