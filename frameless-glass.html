Ultra-clear structural glass balustrades with minimal hardware for unobstructed views and modern aesthetics.

FRAMELESS GLASS

Spigot railing system (SS)

A stainless steel spigot railing system uses small, robust stainless steel posts (spigots) embedded directly into the floor to hold frameless glass panels, creating a highly minimalist and transparent safety barrier with excellent durability and corrosion resistance.

Balustrade railing system

A balustrade railing system is a safety barrier composed of balusters (vertical posts) topped by a handrail, commonly used on stairs, balconies, and landings to prevent falls while adding architectural detail.

Fascia mount railing system

A frameless fascia mount railing system is a sleek, glass-only railing where panels are attached to the side (fascia) of a structure using hidden or minimal fixings, providing unobstructed views and maximizing floor space without visible posts.

Wood balustrade railing system

A wood balustrade railing system is a classic safety barrier made from wooden balusters (vertical posts) and a wooden handrail, commonly used on stairs and balconies to provide support, safety, and a warm, traditional aesthetic.
