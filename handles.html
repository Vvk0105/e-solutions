HANDLES

Pull handle

Pull handles are fixed, non-turning handles designed for pulling doors or drawers open, commonly used on entry doors, cabinets, and appliances for straightforward operation.

Lever handle

Lever handles are turning handles with a lever-like arm that, when pressed down, retract a latch to open a door, offering easy operation, especially useful for accessibility.

Luxuary lever handle

Luxury lever handles are high-end turning handles crafted from premium materials with exquisite finishes and ergonomic designs, offering a sophisticated aesthetic and enhanced tactile experience for doors.
