ALUMINIUM DOORS

PT doors (Pivot and Slide Doors)

A type of door system that combines both sliding and pivoting mechanisms on a vertical axis rather than traditional hinges, creating a dramatic and contemporary entrance with a wide, smooth opening. They are known for their sleek design and ability to handle large, heavy panels.

Frame Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Aluminium Mill Finish
  • Silver Finish
  • (Custom finishes available)

Glass Options:

  • Single Glazed (Toughened/Tempered)
  • Laminated
  • Frosted
  • Performance

Sliding/Swing Doors

Aluminium sliding doors glide horizontally on tracks, offering space-saving operation and maximizing views and natural light. Aluminium swing doors open and close on hinges, similar to traditional doors, providing a classic entry solution with durability and versatility.

  • Black Matte

  • Silver Finish

  • Performance

Telescopic doors

Aluminium telescopic doors are multi-panel sliding doors that stack compactly behind each other as they open, providing maximum clear opening width for large spaces while saving significant wall space compared to traditional sliding doors.

Frame Finish:

  • Black Matte
  • Silver
  • Grey Matte
  • Brushed Gold
  • (Custom finishes available)

Glass Options:

  • Single Glazed (Toughened/Tempered)
  • Laminated
  • Frosted
  • Performance

Sliding folding doors

Aluminium sliding folding doors consist of multiple panels that slide along a track and fold up like an accordion, neatly stacking to one or both sides to create wide, unobstructed openings.
